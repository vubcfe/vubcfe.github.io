I am a Lecturer and Researcher in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at FPT University, Da Nang, Vietnam, which I joined in May 2024. I am an engineer by training, and my research asks a measurement question: how wrong is a map or an estimate produced by spatial AI, why, and how can we know before it is used?

I study this question through multi-scale remote sensing for smart agriculture in Vietnam, combining Sentinel satellite data with UAV imagery. Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 time series give wide coverage for crop mapping and yield estimation, starting with coffee in the Central Highlands, and errors and uncertainty are checked against field data. I am now adding UAV imagery, which gives centimetre-level detail and 3D crop structure, builds on my Ph.D. work in 3D reconstruction, and will serve as a reference with known error for satellite models. I also study how compression and on-device processing affect map accuracy. I also work on industry projects: an ultra-wideband (UWB) project with Qorvo and FPT Software, and SkyCadrAI, which applies AI to PCB layout.

Before FPT University, I worked in France from 2014 to 2023 as a 3D computer vision engineer at Levels3D in Troyes and LTU in Paris, on real-time indoor 3D reconstruction, point cloud segmentation and 3D object retrieval. I completed my Ph.D. in Engineering Science at the Université de Technologie de Troyes in 2018 under an industrial CIFRE agreement.

See my [projects](/projects/) for the applied work, or the full list of [publications](/publications/).
