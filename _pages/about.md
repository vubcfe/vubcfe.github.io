I am a Lecturer and Researcher in the Department of Artificial Intelligence at FPT University, Da Nang, Vietnam, which I joined in May 2024. I take an applied approach to AI: my work starts from a problem in the field or in industry, and the goal is a system that people can use.

My main area is satellite imagery for agriculture, including crop and land cover mapping from Sentinel-2 data, image super-resolution, and reliable datasets and tools for Vietnamese farmland. I also work on industry projects, such as an ultra-wideband (UWB) project with Qorvo and FPT Software, and SkyCadrAI, which applies AI to PCB layout.

Before FPT University, I worked in France from 2014 to 2023 as a 3D computer vision engineer at Levels3D in Troyes and LTU in Paris, on real-time indoor 3D reconstruction, point cloud segmentation and 3D object retrieval. I completed my Ph.D. in Engineering Science at the Université de Technologie de Troyes in 2018 under an industrial CIFRE agreement.

See my [projects](/projects/) for the applied work, or the full list of [publications](/publications/).
