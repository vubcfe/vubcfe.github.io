CloviX is a practical product where I apply my research. It combines satellite imagery over a growing region with UAV, camera and in-field sensor data where more detail is needed, to produce plot-level crop maps, starting with coffee in the Central Highlands of Vietnam.

It follows the same question as my research: how reliable each map and estimate is, and how to check that before the data are used.
