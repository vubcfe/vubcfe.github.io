CloviX is a startup I am building to bring my research into practice. It is a digital agriculture platform based on a layered-data principle: satellite imagery as a base layer over a whole growing region, with UAV, camera and in-field sensor data added only where more detail is needed. The goal is plot-level crop maps, disease alerts and yield estimates for buyers, exporters, cooperatives, banks and insurers, starting with coffee in the Central Highlands of Vietnam.

The platform follows the same question as my research: how reliable each map and estimate is, and how that reliability can be checked before the data are used.
